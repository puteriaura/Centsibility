# Centsibility Feature Overview

## 👨‍🚀 Parent / Commander Features

1. **Dashboard & Fleet Management**
   - **Overview:** Monitor all kids' Core (RM), Growth (RM), and Stardust (Points) balances in real-time.
   - **Quick Actions:** Easily "Transmit" (Deposit) or "Recall" (Withdraw) funds from a child's account.
   - **Flight Log:** View the recent transaction history for each child.
2. **Approvals Hub**
   - Review pending requests from kids including Qard (loans), reward redemptions, and standard deposit/withdrawal requests.
   - Approve or decline requests with one click (funds are automatically credited/deducted).
3. **Auto-Hibah (Scheduled Allowances)**
   - Create recurring automated allowances (daily, weekly, monthly, etc.) to enforce consistent saving habits.
   - Cancel or manage active schedules at any time.
4. **Child Management & Security**
   - Add new kids or edit existing profiles (name, username, birth year).
   - Secure the parent portal behind a PIN code to prevent unauthorized access by kids.
5. **Reports & Statements**
   - Generate comprehensive monthly financial statements.
   - Export statements as PDF documents.
6. **Growth Configuration**
   - Adjust Hibah (profit sharing) rates for kids' investment balances.

---

## 👩‍🚀 Kid / Crew Member Features

1. **Kid Dashboard**
   - View their total wealth spread across Core (wallet), Growth (investments), and Stardust (reward points).
   - See their Trust Score and recent transactions.
2. **Financial Goals**
   - Create savings goals (e.g., "New Bike") with emojis and target amounts.
   - Track progress visually and automatically refund excess savings if a goal target is lowered.
3. **Growth & Investments**
   - Move money into a Growth account to earn Halal profit over time.
   - View a dynamic 7-day chart tracking investment performance.
4. **Daily Quests (Quiz)**
   - Answer 5 daily educational questions about Islamic finance and general knowledge.
   - Earn Stardust points (+100 pts per correct answer) to spend on rewards.
5. **Qard (Loans)**
   - Request interest-free loans for specific amounts and durations.
   - Includes a digital signature canvas. 
   - View loan agreements as PDF documents.
6. **Sadaqah (Charity)**
   - Dedicate a portion of savings to charity.
   - Track total Sadaqah contributions over time.
7. **Rewards Store**
   - Spend earned Stardust points on custom rewards requested from parents.
8. **Wallet & Customization**
   - Change profile avatars and view personal stats.
